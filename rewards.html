Rewards FAQ

Sign up for free through the JINYA app or at orderjinya.com. All you need is your name, email, and phone number to get started.

You earn points automatically on qualifying purchases made in-restaurant or through online ordering when you're signed in to your account.

Once you have enough points for a reward, it will appear in your account and can be applied to a qualifying order in the app or in-restaurant.

Points and account activity are subject to the terms outlined in the JINYA Rewards program terms and conditions, available in the app.

JINYA Rewards is accepted at participating locations. Check the app or ask your local restaurant to confirm participation.
