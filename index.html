No Ramen, No Life

Slow-Simmered for 20+ Hours

Slowly simmered for more than twenty hours, each of our six signature broths balances flavors from whole pork bones, chicken and vegetables with just the right amount of bonito, dashi, miso, kombu, and other classic Japanese ingredients.

The result is a rich broth that is thick, full-flavored, and unforgettable — paired with handcrafted noodles that undergo a special aging process before they're cooked and served.
