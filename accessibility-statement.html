Accessibility Statement

Last updated: January 1, 2026

Our Commitment

JINYA Holdings, Inc. is committed to ensuring digital accessibility for all guests, including people with disabilities. We are continually improving the user experience for everyone and applying relevant accessibility standards.

Conformance Standard

We aim to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA. These guidelines explain how to make web content more accessible for people with disabilities and more user-friendly for everyone.

Ongoing Efforts

  • Providing descriptive alternative text for meaningful images
  • Ensuring sufficient color contrast between text and background
  • Supporting keyboard navigation throughout the site
  • Using semantic HTML and labeled form fields
  • Testing pages with assistive technologies

Feedback

We welcome your feedback on the accessibility of our website and app. If you encounter an accessibility barrier or have suggestions, please contact us so we can address the issue.
