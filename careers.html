Careers

Join the Real Ramen Culture

JINYA is on a mission to change the way people think about ramen, and it's catching on fast. As JINYA Ramen Bar continues to grow we have many exciting career opportunities available for you. We are always looking for enthusiastic, positive and dependable people to join our team.

Team Member

We're looking for friendly, energetic people to join our team. We offer a variety of training and development opportunities to help you grow within our company. Our Servers, Bussers, Food Runners and Key Hourly Supervisors make up our amazing group of Service Team Members.

Become part of a team where ramen is serious but delicious business. Speed and quality are best friends here at JINYA Ramen Bar and our hourly team members focus on keeping that friendship alive. Exceptional service is a must, from greeting guests, taking and fulfilling their orders, to keeping things organized and clean. The best part? We have fun doing it.

  • A desire to help out where needed and work as part of a team
  • Exceptional service, every shift
  • An appetite for learning
  • A positive attitude and smile
  • Flexibility to work a variety of shifts
General Manager

We're looking for committed professionals who will take full advantage of the chance to lead, grow and succeed. As a JINYA Ramen Bar manager, your decisions and experience will impact your entire team — and can lead to great rewards, including personal growth and competitive salaries.

This position is responsible for the operational excellence of their specified restaurant, continually providing leadership and guidance to the entire management team and hourly staff while promoting all operating standards of JINYA Ramen Bar.

  • Runs shifts effectively to ensure quality products, excellent guest service, and restaurant cleanliness while managing labor and food costs
  • Follows and implements restaurant policies and procedures, holding team members accountable
  • Ensures accurate completion of food safety practices and employment law compliance
  • Coaches and mentors team members to build a high-performing team
  • Responsible for restaurant cash management and inventory monitoring
Kitchen Manager

Our ideal managers have outstanding leadership skills, are guest-focused and ideally proficient in culinary operations. Since we're a mix of fast-casual and fine-dining concept, we're looking for leaders who understand that speed and quality must go hand in hand in order to keep our guests coming back.

"We believe that service comes from the mind — hospitality comes from the heart!"

  • Runs shifts effectively to ensure quality products, guest service, service times, and restaurant cleanliness
  • Ensures proper product quality control, presentation, and order assembly meet system standards
  • Completes food inventory orders accurately using forecasts and current sales
  • Delegates and ensures completion of Quality Control/Safe Food checklists

College graduate with a degree in hotel and restaurant management, or equivalent experience, preferred.
