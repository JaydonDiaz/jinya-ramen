Nutritional Information

Every bowl is made fresh in-house, which means nutritional values can vary by location and preparation. Browse menu categories below, and see the disclaimer for how to get detailed allergen data.

Nutrition Disclaimer

Nutritional values are estimates based on standard recipes and preparation methods. Actual values may vary due to differences in suppliers, ingredient substitutions, portion sizes, and regional or seasonal menu variations across locations.

Allergen Disclaimer

Our kitchens handle wheat, soy, shellfish, fish, egg, dairy, tree nuts, and peanuts. While we take care in food handling, we cannot guarantee that any item is completely free of allergens due to shared kitchen equipment and preparation areas. Guests with food allergies or sensitivities should speak with restaurant staff before ordering, and use their own judgment when deciding what and whether to eat.
