Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2026

Information We Collect How We Use It Sharing Your Choices Security Children's Privacy Contact Us

JINYA Holdings, Inc. and its affiliated restaurants ("JINYA," "we," "us," or "our") respect your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and share information when you visit our restaurants, use our website, place an order online, use our mobile app, or join JINYA Rewards.

Information We Collect

We may collect information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, phone number, delivery address, payment information, and date of birth when you place an order, create an account, join JINYA Rewards, or contact us. We may also automatically collect information about your device and how you interact with our website and app, including IP address, browser type, and pages visited.

How We Use Your Information

  • To process and fulfill orders placed online, in-app, or in-restaurant
  • To administer the JINYA Rewards program and deliver rewards, offers, and promotions
  • To respond to inquiries submitted through our contact, careers, or franchise forms
  • To improve our website, app, menu, and guest experience
  • To comply with legal obligations and protect against fraud

Sharing Your Information

We may share information with service providers who help us operate our business (such as payment processors and delivery partners), with individual JINYA franchise locations relevant to your order or inquiry, and as required by law. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Your Choices

You may update your account information at any time through the JINYA app, unsubscribe from marketing emails using the link in any message, and request access to or deletion of your personal information by contacting us. Depending on where you live, additional rights may apply — see our Your Privacy Choices and Opt-Out Preferences pages.

Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect your information. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children's Privacy

Our website, app, and JINYA Rewards program are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us or write to us at 21045 Erwin Street, Suite 1A, Woodland Hills, CA 91367.
